# Convergence and Shear

**▶ Open it: [jameshhchan.github.io/convergence-shear](https://jameshhchan.github.io/convergence-shear/)**

An interactive diagram of how convergence κ and shear γ turn a circular source into an elliptical lensed image.

Use the sliders to set κ, the shear strength |γ| and its orientation φγ. The left panel shows the image elongated along the tangential direction e<sub>t</sub>. The right panel shows the shear as a vector at twice that angle, 2φγ. The page also shows γ₁ and γ₂, the eigenvalues λ<sub>t</sub> = 1 − κ − |γ| and λ<sub>n</sub> = 1 − κ + |γ|, the reduced shear g = γ/(1 − κ), and the axis ratio q = (1 − |g|)/(1 + |g|).

Runs in any modern browser; nothing to install.
